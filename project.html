Project Overview & Training Description

To supplement my academic studies in Information Systems & Analytics and reinforce my daily responsibilities at the LSU LONI SOC, I actively engage in hands-on training through TryHackMe. TryHackMe provides interactive, virtualized environments that allow practitioners to experiment with realistic systems, explore cybersecurity concepts, and solve technical challenges.

Through guided rooms and structured learning paths, I work directly with virtual machines and target systems to examine cybersecurity principles from an applied perspective. This practical approach enables me to test foundational concepts in real time, analyze system configurations, and understand how defensive controls operate within modern operating systems and networks.

Rather than relying solely on theoretical course material, completing these interactive labs helps bridge the gap between classroom knowledge and operational execution, building intuition for system investigation, log interpretation, and threat mitigation.

What I Learned

1. Bridging Theory with Applied Execution

Reading about cybersecurity concepts in textbooks provides important foundational context, but navigating an actual virtual machine and inspecting running services makes the concepts concrete. Hands-on labs demonstrate exactly how misconfigurations and weaknesses manifest in operational systems.

2. The Value of Methodical Investigation

Solving guided room tasks requires patience, close attention to detail, and a structured approach. I learned the importance of reading system output, verifying assumptions, checking error codes, and documenting each step taken rather than guessing solutions.

3. Defensive Awareness & Threat Context

Experiencing both guided attacks and defense scenarios deepens my appreciation for secure configuration, robust logging, and swift alert triage. Understanding how security gaps are identified reinforces my day-to-day focus on monitoring and protecting networks at the SOC.

4. Consistency & Continuous Growth

Cybersecurity is an evolving field where persistent practice is essential. Committing to self-paced learning paths on TryHackMe fosters an agile mindset, encouraging me to research unfamiliar technologies and build practical confidence step by step.
